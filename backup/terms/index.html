Supernova Backup

Terms of use

A utility for the owner's personal use.

Purpose

Supernova Backup connects the owner's systems to the owner's Google Drive for backup and recovery tasks. It is not offered as a public backup service, and visitors should not submit files or credentials through this website.

Account and backup management

The owner is responsible for the connected Google account, the files selected for backup, available storage, retention settings and recovery checks. Google Drive and rclone remain subject to their own applicable terms and licences.

Availability and recovery

Transfers may fail because of connectivity, quota, storage or authorisation issues. A successful transfer does not by itself establish that every required file is backed up or that a complete restoration will succeed. Independent copies and recovery checks remain necessary.

Stopping use

The owner can stop scheduled jobs and revoke Google access. Existing backup copies remain until removed through the owner's storage and retention controls.
