Supernova Backup

Privacy policy

This utility is for the owner's personal backups.

Information accessed and used

The Google Drive connection can access file contents and metadata, including names, sizes, modification times and checksums. It uses this access to list backup folders, upload selected backup files, verify transfers and remove older backup copies under the owner's retention settings.

The requested full Drive permission technically allows access beyond backup folders. The utility is intended to use that access only for the owner's backup operations.

Storage and sharing

Backup files are stored in the owner's Google Drive. Local copies, connection credentials and operational logs are held on the owner's systems. Logs may include file names, transfer status and error information. Credentials and backup contents are not published on this information website.

Google receives the files and metadata needed to provide Drive storage and authorisation. Google user data accessed through this utility is not sold, used for advertising, shared with AI providers or used to train general-purpose AI models.

Retention and deletion

The owner manages backup retention. Older cloud backups may be deleted automatically after a successful backup. The owner can also remove stored backup files and local copies directly; revoking access does not itself delete existing copies.

Your control

The owner can stop the backup job and revoke the app's access in Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorised access once it takes effect.

Website visitors

These information pages contain no sign-in form, embedded analytics scripts or advertising. The hosting and network providers may process routine request information to deliver and protect the website.
